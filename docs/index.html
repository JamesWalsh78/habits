Overview

Autumn reset, tracked simply

This placeholder overview focuses on consistency: eat within target calories and macros, hydrate well, train often, keep steps up, read, practice Spanish, sleep earlier, and protect time from screens.

What the plan is trying to do

The aim is a balanced cut and performance routine rather than an extreme program. Each day combines enough fuel, enough protein, structured exercise, steady hydration, and calm routines that make the rest of the day easier to win.

Daily scorecard

Every metric is graded red, amber, or green. The whole day turns green when at least 90% of metrics are green, amber when at least 75% are green or amber, and red otherwise.

Core anchors

  • Calories and macros keep nutrition tight without being rigid.
  • Water, steps, and exercise support energy and recovery.
  • Reading and Spanish keep momentum outside the gym.
  • Earlier wake and bed times reinforce the whole plan.

How to use it

  • Pick a date and fill the form once per day.
  • Choose an existing date to edit that day's entry.
  • Use Results to review totals, trends, and consistency.
  • Export to CSV or JSON whenever you want a backup or an Excel import.
